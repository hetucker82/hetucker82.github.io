Research

My research centers the lived experiences of people facing intersecting forms of stigma, and the ways communities navigate it through resistance, resilience, community building, and the making of space for one another.

My work focuses on intervention development, using participatory methods to design responses with and for the people most affected, grounded in an understanding of how structural conditions shape HIV prevention and care, sexual and reproductive health, mental health, and experiences of violence. Over more than a decade I have carried out qualitative and ethnographic research with people living with and vulnerable to HIV, much of it in sub Saharan Africa, and my recent work extends these questions to climate and health, tracing how extreme weather compounds the conditions people already live within.

Research threads

  • Stigma, structure, and HIVCommunity centered qualitative research on how people living with and vulnerable to HIV navigate intersecting forms of stigma and the structural barriers that shape access to prevention and care.
  • Sexual and reproductive health and rightsWork on HIV and sexual and reproductive health barriers, gender based violence, and the conditions people need in order to make decisions about sex, reproduction, and care.
  • Mental health and community led interventionAdapting, implementing, and evaluating peer delivered mental health support with community partners in Kenya.
  • Self administered and long acting preventionHow tools such as PrEP, the ring, implants, and self testing can widen access to HIV and STI prevention and care, and for whom.
  • Climate, health, and bodily autonomyHow extreme weather, from floods and typhoons to drought and heat, reshapes HIV care, mental health, and people's capacity to decide about their own bodies, including collaborative work at UCSF.
  • Participatory and feminist methodsFeminist participatory research, ethnography, and digital ethnography as tools for building interventions alongside communities.

About

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Training in AIDS Prevention Studies program at the UCSF Center for AIDS Prevention Studies. I hold a PhD in Gender Studies with a focus in sociocultural anthropology from Central European University, an MPH in Epidemiology from UC Berkeley, and an MA in Gender and International Development from the Institute of Development Studies at the University of Sussex. Before UCSF I was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Michigan Center for Global Health Equity.
